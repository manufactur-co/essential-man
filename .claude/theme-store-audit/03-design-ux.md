# 3. Theme Design and UX

**Source:** https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/themes/store/requirements#theme-design-and-ux

Themes must meet every checkpoint in this section. Covers visual quality, layout, consistency, shopping experience, and demo store realism.

## Visual Design and Art Direction

- [ ] Unique, intentional design clearly targeting a specific merchant type/industry
- [ ] All images, graphics, and icons are high-quality, clear, appropriately sized, and consistent
- [ ] Cohesive, complementary color palette — no clashing or reduced readability

## Layout

- [ ] Logical grid structure with deliberate, consistent spacing and alignment
- [ ] Clear content hierarchy — size, color, contrast, and position guide user attention
- [ ] Layouts remain visually balanced when content length, image count, or text quantity varies

## Consistency

- [ ] No overabundance of fonts; consistent font pairing throughout the theme
- [ ] Buttons, links, and forms use consistent styles, sizes, colors, and behaviors everywhere
- [ ] Theme editor settings are merchant-friendly, logically grouped, and discoverable
- [ ] Theme editor customization is intuitive and merchant-first

## Customer Shopping Experience

- [ ] Clear navigation: homepage → product discovery → PDP → cart → checkout
- [ ] Thoughtful product discovery (menus, featured collections, recommendations)
- [ ] Key actions (variant selection, ATC, quantity edit, checkout) are clear and immediately responsive

## Demo Store

- [ ] No Lorem Ipsum or placeholder content — fully realistic demo store
- [ ] All sections shown explicitly fit the type of business portrayed
- [ ] Inspiring with engaging content and realistic, intentional product displays
